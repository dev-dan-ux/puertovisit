About Puerto Galera

Puerto Galera is a municipality on the northern coast of Oriental Mindoro. It is known for sheltered bays, white-sand beaches, and reefs that draw divers and snorkelers from across the country. Its marine biodiversity earned it recognition as a UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve.

Galera Escapes was created to make the destination easier to enjoy. We plan the boat, the guide, and the schedule so you can focus on the water.

Location: Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro, Philippines.

Target market: young professionals, barkadas, families, and first-time visitors from Luzon.

Our mission

Deliver safe, fairly priced island experiences that keep more tourism income in the local community.

Our vision

A Puerto Galera where visitors and residents look after the reefs together.

Why choose us

Small groups (max 10), licensed boat operators, life vests for everyone, and a reef-friendly briefing before every swim.
