Travel guide

Best time to visit

March to May for calm, sunny seas. June to November is the wet season and boat trips may be cancelled for safety.

What to bring

Sunscreen (reef-safe), hat, swimwear, rash guard, towel, dry bag, cash, and a reusable water bottle.

Safety and etiquette

Wear your life vest, follow guide instructions, and dress modestly when visiting villages. Ask before taking photos of people.

Environment

Do not touch or stand on corals, feed fish, or leave trash. Take your waste back to shore.

Estimated budget

PHP 3,000 to 6,000 per person for a weekend, including transport, food, and one tour.

Emergency

Call 911 nationwide. For local help, contact the Puerto Galera Municipal Tourism Office or the nearest health center.

Accessibility

Boat boarding involves steps and uneven sand. Tell us in your inquiry if anyone needs assistance, and we will plan a suitable route.

Frequently asked questions

How do I confirm my booking?

Send an inquiry below. We reply within 24 hours with availability and a quote. A booking is confirmed once you reply to accept.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Trips may be cancelled when the coast guard advises against sailing. We will reschedule you or refund your deposit.

Are children allowed?

Yes. Children join with an adult and use child-size life vests.

Is the ferry included?

No. Ferry tickets from Batangas are booked separately. We can share the schedule.
