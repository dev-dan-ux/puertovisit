Getting to Puerto Galera

From Metro Manila

  1. Travel to Batangas Pier by bus or van (about 2.5 to 3 hours from Manila, traffic permitting).
  2. Take a ferry or fast craft to Balatero or Muelle Pier in Puerto Galera (about 1 to 1.5 hours).
  3. Ride a tricycle or jeepney to your hotel or to our meeting point.

Nearby attractions: White Beach, Sabang, Tamaraw Falls, Talipanan Beach.

Where to stay and eat: guesthouses and resorts in White Beach and Sabang; grill restaurants and cafes along the beachfront. We can suggest options in your budget.
